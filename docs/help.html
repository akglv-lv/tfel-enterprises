FAQ

Confirm with Tyler
Why does my order take a few days to ship?

Every piece is printed for you when you order it. That takes a little longer than grabbing it off a shelf, but it means nothing is overproduced and every size and colour stays in stock.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Message us as soon as possible with your order number. Once an order has gone to print, it can't be changed.

How do I track my order?

You'll get an email with a tracking link when your order ships.

Do you ship outside the US?

We ship to the US and Canada. Prices on this site are shown in US dollars.

How do I wash it?

Turn it inside out, wash cold and hang dry or tumble dry low. Don't iron over the print.

Shipping

Confirm with Tyler

Orders are printed within 2 to 5 business days, then shipped. US delivery usually takes 3 to 7 business days after that, and Canada 5 to 10.

Shipping cost is worked out at checkout from your address and what's in your cart.

Returns and exchanges

Confirm with Tyler

Because every piece is made to order, we don't take returns for a change of mind or the wrong size. Check the size guide below before you order.

If something arrives damaged, misprinted or wrong, message us within 30 days with your order number and a photo, and we'll send a replacement or refund.
